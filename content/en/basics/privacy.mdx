## Scope and Contact

The Encinet team is responsible for personal information processed through the Mik Casual Minecraft server, website, and the QQ and Matrix community features it operates. Data processed independently by third-party platforms is covered by their own privacy policies.

For privacy questions or data requests, privately contact a Custodian in our [community](/en/join). Include your player name or UUID if the request concerns player data.

## Data and Uses

| Area | Data and Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Minecraft multiplayer and saves | IP addresses and connection information support connections and identity checks; UUIDs, player names, and skins identify and display players. Position, inventory, ender chest, progress, and statistics preserve your character's state. Builds, terrain, containers, entities, signs, and books are saved with the world |
| Server features | QQ or Matrix account identifiers, membership, and account links verify admission. Data comes from your connection, game and website activity, submissions, and game authentication and community platforms. Admission requires at least one valid QQ or Matrix account link.

We do not sell personal information. We process data necessary to provide the service to fulfill our service agreement, and data required by law to meet legal obligations. Where applicable law permits, we may rely on legitimate interests to process data needed for security, rule enforcement, and appeals. We obtain consent in advance where legally required.

## Communication and Visibility

- **Public chat** syncs between Minecraft, the web, and Matrix and is written to console logs. Messages from QQ Group 1 sync to these public channels; public messages sent to QQ must start with `#` and pass moderation. See the [bridging rules](?section=chat#platform-bridging).
- **Private messages and staff chat** are delivered to the two participants and authorized staff, respectively. Neither is forwarded to QQ groups or Matrix rooms.
- **Voice chat** is transmitted to listeners through Plasmo Voice.
- **Public information** includes player names, UUIDs, skins, online status, and the time each player joined their current session. It is available through the website and public API without signing in. Build information, credits, and public sanctions and governance outcomes are displayed under the [server rules](?section=rules).

Web chat uses application-layer encryption between the browser and game server. Message content is encrypted before reaching Cloudflare's proxy. Cloudflare can see network information such as IP addresses and connection times but does not hold the application-layer keys needed to decrypt message content.

The game server decrypts and processes messages and stores chat history in plaintext. Maintainers with host, database, or backup access can read these records.

## Account Security

- **Identity verification.** Java Edition uses Minecraft's official account authentication. Bedrock Edition uses Xbox / Microsoft account authentication and requires a linked Java account. The server also requires an additional password check.
- **Password protection.** Additional passwords are stored as salted hashes; maintainers cannot retrieve the original password. Authorized staff who reset a password can see the newly generated password. Players should sign in and change it themselves as soon as possible after receiving it.
- **Website login.** Sign in using a temporary code confirmed in the game or a Passkey. Passkey private keys stay on your device or in your password manager. The website stores only public keys and credential identifiers, and does not receive fingerprints, face data, or device PINs.

Manage Passkeys and website sessions in [Account Security](/en/account/security). Revoking a Passkey does not sign out existing website sessions.

These services may process data internationally. Ask our privacy contact about processing locations and transfer safeguards.

## Browser Data and Retention

Website login uses necessary cookies. Theme and notification preferences stay in the browser.

| Data | Retention or Validity |
| --- | --- |
| Character saves, progress, statistics, and shared world | Kept as needed to run the game and maintain the world |
| Chat database history | Normally retained for **7 days after recording** |
| Website sessions | Expire after **30 days of inactivity** or **90 days from issue**; can be revoked earlier |
| Login and account linking codes | Normally valid for **5 minutes** |
| Official authentication cache | Valid for **72 hours** after successful official authentication, used only during an official authentication outage to verify reconnections from the same IP |
| Submission review records | **14 days after review is completed**; published builds and hosted images are stored separately |
| Browser chat history | No automatic expiry by default. Chat settings let you clear history or disable saving and clear it; signing out or switching accounts also clears it |
| Account links | Kept until unlinked |
| AI knowledge and memories | No automatic expiry; deletion can be requested |

Console logs, system backups, and messages already synced to third-party platforms are retained separately. Clearing history from the chat database does not automatically delete those copies.

## Minors' Privacy

Where a guardian's consent is legally required, we explain the processing and obtain valid consent beforehand.

Report and appeal materials involving minors are shared only with necessary case handlers. Private information is redacted from published outcomes.

Minors and their guardians can submit privacy requests through the contact channel above. We verify identity and guardianship as needed, taking the minor's wishes and interests into account.

## Privacy Requests and Updates

You can request access to or a copy of your personal information, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, data portability where available by law, or withdrawal of consent. You can also object to processing based on legitimate interests where provided by law. We verify the requester's identity and handle requests under applicable law.

Information that qualifies for deletion will be deleted from active systems or anonymized. This does not necessarily include builds in the shared world, public projects, or other participants' work.

Historical backups may not be immediately erasable. We will explain how and when we expect to clear the data, restrict its routine use until then, and apply the relevant deletion or anonymization measures when restoring a backup. Deletion may be limited for information that must be retained by law or is necessary to handle an ongoing dispute.

Requests are normally free. We usually respond within **one calendar month of receipt**, or sooner where required by law. If a legally permitted extension is needed, we will promptly explain the reason and expected response date. You can also complain to a privacy regulator with jurisdiction.

The system determines admission, promotion, and governance eligibility using account links, verification, and play records. Messages forwarded to QQ undergo content moderation. You can request human review if you dispute an automated result.

If a personal data security incident requires notification by law, we will inform affected users of its impact and corrective measures. Material policy changes will be announced in advance, and we will seek consent separately where legally required.
