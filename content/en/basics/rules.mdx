## Basic Principles

Mik Casual is a non-profit creative Minecraft server focused on building and relaxed play.

1. Players may build freely, use utility tools, express opinions, and contribute to public projects, but may not harm other people's work, normal play, dignity, or server safety.
2. These rules apply in the game and community spaces operated by the server. In each community, also follow its published supplementary rules.
3. Conduct not prohibited by these rules is generally allowed. New rules do not punish completed conduct retroactively.

## Roles and Promotion

| Role | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `Newcomer` | May build and chat; automatically promoted to Member when eligible |
| `Member` | Broader building and interaction permissions; active Members may stand for Moderator and vote |
| `Moderator` | Appointed by vote to enforce rules and handle disputes and emergencies |
| `Custodian` | Responsible for server operation, account and data security; may exercise Moderator authority |

A Newcomer is promoted automatically after **3 days** from first joining and **8 hours** of active play across at least **2 calendar days**, unless an active ban or promotion hold applies.

Active play is non-AFK time recorded by the system. Do not fake activity to evade AFK detection. Those periods do not count toward active play or event rewards.

## Play and Communication

### Player Interaction

1. Do not forcibly move or teleport other players, or change their game modes, without consent.
2. Do not obstruct another player's building, movement, or controls through close following, blocking, collisions, or entities.
3. Do not use jukeboxes, note blocks, voice, or other sound sources to persistently disrupt others' building, conversation, or rest.
4. After someone refuses, do not repeatedly send teleport requests, mentions, invitations, or direct messages to disrupt or force a response.

### Expression and Content

1. Players may express political positions, social views, criticism, and disagreement.
2. Sustained or severe insults, personal attacks, and malicious provocation are prohibited. Threats and targeted harassment are prohibited, as are discriminatory degradation and incitement of harm based on race, ethnicity, nationality, region, sex, sexual orientation, disability, religion, or a comparable identity.
3. Do not flood public spaces, send repeated or unsolicited unrelated advertising or commercial promotion, or maliciously divert traffic.
4. Do not knowingly spread false allegations likely to harm players or the community.
5. Pornography, sexually explicit content (R18), and extreme gore (R18G) are prohibited.
6. Do not request sexual content from minors, send it to them, or induce them to produce it, including through direct messages or contact on other platforms.

### Privacy and Safety

1. **Personal information.** Do not disclose another person's non-public personal information, such as their real identity, home address, contact details, network address, or account credentials.
2. **Accounts and devices.** Do not impersonate others to deceive people, or send phishing, malicious downloads, or other content that endangers accounts or devices.
3. **Permissions and data.** Do not bypass permissions or protection mechanisms, or access or export non-public server data, without authorization. Stop using security vulnerabilities and report them privately.
4. **Performance and load.** Redstone devices, entity buildup, chunk loading, or high-frequency bulk operations capable of causing material lag, crashes, or data loss are prohibited. Disable or reduce devices and operations that cause material performance problems.

## Building and Maintenance

### Builds, Terrain, and Collaboration

1. **Respect other people's work.** Altering another person's build or its supporting terrain requires the author's consent or permission under these rules.
2. **Protect terrain and finish the site.** Keep terrain changes proportionate to construction needs, then restore the terrain or finish natural transitions.
3. **Credit builders and sources.** Consider crediting the builders on a sign near the build or in its description. When referencing, recreating, or adapting someone else's work, credit the original author and source. Do not claim others' work as your own, remove their credit, or conceal a collaborator's major contribution.

### Siting and Coordination

Registered areas should be proportionate to construction plans. Do not claim others' work or block public access.

Approved public projects follow their plans. Impacts on the surroundings not covered by the plan, and any unresolved disputes, follow this section's rules.

1. **Coordinate beforehand.** If construction affects nearby works, daylight, access, or the use of public space, discuss it with the affected players before work begins.
2. **High-impact construction.** Any of the following requires a plan approved by a Moderator or Custodian before construction:
   - Extensive overhead coverage or obstruction that substantially reduces daylight below;
   - Obstruction of existing roads, primary entrances, or necessary routes;
   - Substantial interference with the existing use of public space or a principal sightline deliberately framed by a build.
3. **Disputes and construction.** Obtain the necessary author consent before construction. Do not work on disputed parts until the dispute is resolved by agreement or a ruling explicitly permits the work.

### Public Projects and Maintenance

Public areas and facilities are intended for shared community activities, transport, or services. Their scope is confirmed by a Moderator or Custodian.

1. Members may carry out routine maintenance in public areas and on public facilities, preserving their existing layout and style.
2. Construction, overall changes, or continuation of public projects require a plan approved by a Moderator or Custodian before work begins. If existing works are affected, the plan must explain how they will be handled, and author credits must be preserved.

### Stalled Private Projects

If an unfinished private project has made no substantive progress for **90 days** and obstructs others, a Moderator or Custodian notifies the author and coordinates how to address the obstructive part, giving priority to workable plans to resume construction. Do not alter disputed parts until the dispute is resolved by agreement or a ruling explicitly permits it.

## Licensing and Website Display

### Copyright and Licensing

1. Copyright and other lawful rights that arise in a work remain with the relevant creators.
2. Original material newly created on the server after this provision is published is released by default under **[CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/)** where its creator has authority to license it, unless rights were reserved beforehand under item 3. Where another person's consent is required to license the work, the default applies only with that consent. Older works are not licensed retroactively; existing licenses remain valid.
3. Before substantive creation, authors may reserve rights for later versions in the work's description or a server community message. Identify the work, author, location, and scope, and retain a record of the declaration. Existing CC licenses and the operational license in item 7 remain valid.
4. Sharing or adapting CC work requires credit, the source, a license link, and modification notices, following the license's NonCommercial and ShareAlike terms. Commercial use requires separate permission. Do not impose additional restrictions on licensed rights.
5. Third-party works and material a builder cannot license are excluded from the default license. Collaborative work is covered only where each creator has authority and the licenses are compatible; mark differing or unclear licenses as mixed rights.
6. A license covers only the relevant work, not the whole world save or collection, and does not authorize editing the original build on the server.
7. To the extent permitted by law, creators grant the server a non-exclusive, royalty-free operational license to store, render, back up, restore, and migrate works, and to maintain and adjust public areas and facilities under these rules. It lasts while the work remains on the server or in reasonable backups, even after the author leaves. It does not include selling the work externally or licensing its creative portion to third parties.

### Featuring Builds on the Website

1. The website features only reasonably complete works that follow these rules and have clear rights information.
2. Obtain the author's express consent before featuring a work, and identify major contributors to group works.
3. Identify authors, creation date, source, license, and modifications, distinguishing original work, recreations, and adaptations.
4. Authors may request removal from the website.
5. Delay display or hide an existing feature while ownership, sources, or display permission remain uncertain, pending review.

## Community Governance

### Active Members

The system calculates **active Member** eligibility for Moderator registration and governance votes. A Member must meet all of these conditions:

- Have held Member status for at least **30 days**;
- Have at least **6 hours** of active play during the past **60 days** (**12 hours** when eligibility is assessed from **Jan 1 to Mar 15** or **Jun 25 to Sep 15** each year, inclusive), spread across at least **6 qualifying participation days**;
- Have at least **2 qualifying participation days** during the past **30 days**; and
- Have no active ban.

A calendar day qualifies after **30 minutes** of active play.

### Moderator Appointments and Recalls

Each person may use only one Member account to stand for Moderator and vote. Appointments and recalls use a **3-day** secret vote, with the voter roll fixed when voting begins. Publish necessary information about appointment and recall results.

1. Active Members may stand for Moderator, with a separate vote for each candidate. Withdrawal or loss of eligibility before voting closes ends that appointment.
2. Yes votes must reach **two thirds** of non-abstaining ballots and exceed **one half of all participating ballots**. Abstentions count toward turnout. The following minimums also apply:

| Active Members | Minimum turnout | Minimum yes votes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4 or more | **25%, rounded up**, and at least **3 people** | **3** |
| 2 or 3 | **2 people** | **2** |
| Fewer than 2 | Appointments and recalls cannot begin | N/A |

3. A recall requires at least 3 active Members other than the recall subject to start it jointly. If only 2 other active Members exist, both must start it.
4. A candidate or recall subject may vote but may not administer that vote. If no disinterested Moderator or Custodian is available, the temporary review panel administers it and publishes totals.
5. A Moderator may resign at any time. After **180 consecutive days** without a login, the system removes the longest-absent Moderators until 4 remain.

### Authority and Responsibilities

Moderators and Custodians must recuse themselves from matters involving themselves, their work, or close collaborations. They may not use authority or non-public information for private benefit, to suppress disagreement, or to evade the rules.

Custodians are responsible for the host, critical accounts, costs, and data. They leave when they lose control or complete a handover, and personnel changes are announced. Rules on Moderator appointment, departure, and inactivity do not apply to Custodians.

## Disputes, Enforcement, and Appeals

### Ruling Procedure

1. A disinterested Moderator or Custodian rules on the evidence and gives the parties a chance to respond. State the facts, rules, outcome, duration, and appeal route, and retain a record.
2. If too few are available, publicly draw up to **3** willing, disinterested active Members at random to form a temporary review panel that decides or reviews the matter.
3. Publish necessary information about rulings and appeals. Share evidence involving minors, privacy, or security only with necessary case handlers, and redact it from public results.

### Remedies and Bans

1. Remedies should stop and repair the impact first. They may include a warning, restoration, public correction, mute, or feature restriction. State how long restrictions last.
2. In an emergency, a Moderator or Custodian may disable the relevant device or feature first. Record the scene, notify its owner, and explain the reason and restoration conditions. Restore it promptly once the risk ends.
3. Select the lowest ban level sufficient to control the risk, considering actual impact, intent, repetition, and subsequent repairs:

| Ban severity | Duration | Typical use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `warning` Warning ban | 3 days | A first or low-impact violation that still requires short separation |
| `excusable` Excusable | 30 days | Actual harm with clear mitigating circumstances and active cooperation in repair |
| `minor` Minor violation | 150 days | Deliberate disruption, repeated violations, or clear but repairable damage |
| `severe` Severe violation | 360 days | Serious intentional destruction, a serious threat, targeted harassment, or major impact on server operation |
| `extreme` Extreme violation | 1500 days | Large-scale or coordinated destruction; grave harm to a person, minor, privacy, account, or data safety |

4. Do not evade bans with another account.

### Appeals

A party may appeal a ruling. Moderators or Custodians uninvolved in the original ruling review it; use a temporary panel if too few are available.
