## Player Monitoring

Monitoring switches you to Spectator mode, follows the target with a third-person camera, and shows their name in the action bar.

| Usage | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `/monitor` | Start random rotation, or exit if already monitoring |
| `/monitor <player>` | Watch one player continuously; switching targets keeps your original return location |
| `/monitor stop` | Exit monitoring |

Random rotation changes targets every 30 seconds and visits each eligible player once per round. It avoids consecutive repeats when another target is available. Watching a named player disables automatic rotation and allows you to inspect AFK players.

Your in-world entity is hidden from ordinary players, including ordinary spectators. They cannot locate you through `/tp`, `/teleport`, or the native spectator menu. Your normal online entry remains in Tab; only staff see the localized `[Monitor]` / `[监控]` marker after your name. Monitoring players are also excluded from nearby invisibility notifications.

<Details title="Rotation, AFK, and Exit Rules">

- Random rotation excludes yourself, other monitoring staff, spectators, dead or offline players, and players you cannot see. An AFK player is skipped only after at least 3 minutes without valid input; repeatedly using `/afk` does not grant an immediate exemption.
- Random rotation selects another player when its target becomes unavailable, or waits if no eligible players remain. A fixed target becoming unavailable makes monitoring wait for the same account to return; it does not switch to someone else.
- Monitoring suspends automatic AFK and excludes you from activity rewards. Camera movement does not refresh activity clocks. Starting monitoring while AFK removes AFK protection; manually using `/afk` during monitoring ends the session and retains the AFK state.
- A successful exit restores your original location, game mode, and flight state. AFK checks resume using your existing activity history. Monitoring movement does not enter `/back` history. If the return fails, monitoring remains active; retry with `/monitor stop`.

</Details>

## Investigation and Recovery

### Block Records

Enable the Prism inspector with `/pr wand inspect`, then click at the scene:

- **Left-click** queries the clicked block's location.
- **Right-click a block face** queries the adjacent placement location, useful for inspecting a space where a block was removed.

Use `/pr wand` to turn it off.

<Details title="Filter Records and Preview a Rollback">

Stand at the scene and replace `PlayerName` with the verified account. This example finds that player's block breaks within 10 blocks during the last hour:

```text
/pr lookup p:PlayerName a:break r:10 since:1h -nogroup
```

`-nogroup` shows individual records so you can check their times and locations. Preview using the same player, action, radius, and time filters:

```text
/pr preview-rollback p:PlayerName a:break r:10 since:1h
```

The preview changes only the blocks you see. Use `/pr preview-apply` to apply it, or `/pr preview-cancel` to discard it. Previews cannot display entity or item changes.

`rollback` reverses recorded actions; `restore` reapplies them. Use a rollback to repair broken blocks; `restore` is a different operation.

</Details>

### Inventories and Ender Chests

Use `/openinv <player>` for the target's inventory or `/openender <player>` for their ender chest, then inspect through the inventory interface. Both support offline players.

Taking, placing, or moving items changes the target's actual inventory.

## Server Admission and Manual Unlinking

Server access requires a valid community account link and membership, including for OP accounts. See [Getting Started](?section=getting-started#joining-the-server) for the player workflow.

These administrative commands require `group.custodian` permission and also work in the console.

<Definitions>
<Definition title="Look Up a Player's Links">

Use `/bindadmin lookup <player or UUID>` to view all links for a player. In game, select Unlink on the specific record.

</Definition>
<Definition title="Remove One Selected Link">

Use `/bindadmin unlink <binding ID> confirm`. Console lookup results include the full command. Only the selected record is removed; other platforms and independent scopes are unaffected.

</Definition>
</Definitions>

Once the server confirms an account has left all connected communities on that platform, its link is removed automatically. Manually revoking the last valid link also disconnects an online player. The player must link an account again before rejoining.

## Player Records and Promotion

**Main menu → Community Governance → Player Management.** Search recorded online and offline players by name or UUID, then select a result to view effective playtime, promotion conditions, and pause records.

<Definitions>
<Definition title="Pause Newcomer Promotion">

Choose Pause newcomer promotion, enter a duration, reason, and appeal route, then confirm. Durations accept `m`, `h`, or `d`, such as `2h` or `3d`. Only players who have not become Members can be paused.

</Definition>
<Definition title="Lift Promotion Pause">

Choose Lift promotion pause, enter a reason, and confirm. The original record is retained.

</Definition>
</Definitions>

## Ban Management

Use `/ban` to open the ban menu.

<Steps>
<Step title="Choose the Player">

Search for and select the target, then choose a severity.

</Step>
<Step title="Enter a Reason and Confirm">

Check the displayed target, severity, and expiry, then enter a reason and confirm. Players who have never joined require an additional confirmation. A successful ban disconnects the target's active game connection and publishes a ban notice.

</Step>
</Steps>

See [Disputes, Enforcement, and Appeals](?section=rules#disputes-enforcement-and-appeals) for severity, duration, and procedure.

<Details title="History, Pardons, and Corrections">

The current ban menu creates new bans. These operations still use commands:

| Action | Usage |
| --- | --- |
| View history | `/ban history <player> [page]` |
| Lift the active ban and retain history | `/pardon <player> <reason>` |
| Correct the reason | `/ban edit <player> reason <new reason>` |
| Change severity | `/ban edit <player> severity <severity ID>` |

Severity IDs: `warning`, `excusable`, `minor`, `severe`, `extreme`.

<Callout kind="warning" title="Changing Severity Recalculates Expiry">

Changing the level recalculates the ban's duration from the time of the edit. Check the new expiry before confirming.

</Callout>

</Details>

## Password Resets

Moderators and Custodians need a password before logging in. A newly appointed staff member without one needs another authorized operator to perform a reset.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the Target's Account Security">

Open the target's record in Player Management, then choose Account Security → Reset Password. Offline accounts are supported.

</Step>
<Step title="Confirm and Verify Your Own Password">

Check the target, confirm the reset, and enter your own current password. A successful reset sets a new random password, invalidates the old password, and revokes the target's existing game login.

</Step>
<Step title="Deliver the New Password">

Copy it from the private result form and deliver it through a trusted channel after verifying the account holder. Closing the result makes it unavailable; reset again if you did not obtain it. The player logs in normally and can then change it.

</Step>
</Steps>

### Which Accounts Can a Moderator Reset?

| Target | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Newcomer or Member | Moderator can reset |
| Another Moderator without a password | Moderator can reset |
| Another Moderator with a password | Ask a Custodian to reset |
| Custodian or OP | Use the server console |
| Yourself | Change it through Main menu → Settings → Account Security; if forgotten, ask another authorized operator |

## PVP Management

Use `/pvp admin` to open the online player list.

- Toggle a player's PVP switch directly in the list.
- Open their settings to adjust PVP, mob protection, mounted mob damage, and automatic enabling on death.

Temporary PVP overrides may still determine the effective state; check the displayed state. Managing your own account still respects the combat lock.

## Voice Moderation

Use `/vmute <player> [duration] [reason]` to mute a player through Plasmo Voice, or `/vunmute <player>` to unmute them. This affects voice; text chat is handled separately.

## Staff Chat and Player Locations

`/staff` switches to staff chat; run it again to return to public chat. `/staff <message>` sends one staff message without changing your current channel.

<Details title="Field Teleports">

| Action | Usage |
| --- | --- |
| Visit an online player's current position or an offline player's last saved position | `/tpany <player>` |
| Bring the specified online player to you | `/tpforce <player> @s` |
| Move a player to coordinates | `/tpforce <player> <X> <Y> <Z>` |
| Visit another world | `/mvtp <world>` |

`/tpforce` accepts vanilla `/tp` arguments and bypasses ordinary teleport preferences and consent requests. Other teleport protections may still prevent movement.

`/mvtp` still checks destination permissions and safety.

In Spectator mode, use `/spectate <player>` to follow their view; run `/spectate` without arguments to stop.

## Maintenance and Status

| Usage | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `/maintenance` | Check maintenance mode |
| `/maintenance on` | Restrict new logins to Moderators and Custodians |
| `/maintenance off` | Allow ordinary players to log in again |
| `/network status` | Inspect outbound traffic, throttling stage, view-distance ratios, limited explorers, and server tick time |
| `/viaversion list` | List online players by client version |
| `/plugins` | View the server plugin list |

Maintenance mode does not disconnect players already online. It defaults to off after a server restart.
