## Iron-block elevators

Connect floors using iron blocks, without commands or registration.

### Build an elevator

<Steps>
<Step title="Place iron blocks on each floor">

Keep at least one shared X/Z column between levels.

</Step>
<Step title="Leave a safe landing">

Allow enough standing room above the blocks, without obstacles, liquids, or fire.

</Step>
</Steps>

Solid floors between levels are allowed, with no fixed floor spacing. Side-connected iron blocks at the same height form one platform; diagonal contact does not connect them. Each platform supports up to 64 iron blocks.

### Travel between floors

| Action | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Jump | Travel to the next safe floor above |
| Press sneak | Travel to the next safe floor below; holding sneak does not repeat |
| Aim at a floor number and left- or right-click | Travel directly to that floor |

<Callout title="Your position is preserved">

Travel preserves your X/Z position and view. If your column has no safe landing at a floor, up/down travel searches farther; direct selection is unavailable for that floor.

</Callout>

With at least two standable floors, a personal selection panel appears on a nearby wall or platform edge. The current floor has a green underline; unavailable floors have a yellow strikethrough. Longer lists have pages.

### Mounts and leashed animals

Mounts, passengers, and leash-connected animals can travel together. Group members that are not passengers must stand on the same departure platform. Unconnected nearby animals stay behind. Floors without enough room for the whole group are skipped.

<Details title="Elevator not responding">

- Leave flight, gliding, swimming, or spectator mode and stand on the iron block.
- Press sneak again for another downward trip. Successful travel has a 0.5-second cooldown.
- Discovery may take a moment after entering or changing a platform. Unloaded areas and other plugins' teleport restrictions can also affect travel.

</Details>

## Building and decoration

### WorldEdit

WorldEdit is installed on the server for copying, pasting, rotating structures, and editing terrain. No extra client mod is needed. Operations still depend on your account permissions.

- [Common commands overview (Chinese)](https://www.mcmod.cn/post/3050.html)
- [Full command reference (Chinese)](https://www.mcmod.cn/post/3533.html)
- [FAWE commands and permissions (English)](https://intellectualsites.gitbook.io/fastasyncworldedit/features/main-commands-and-permissions)

### Armor stand editor

Run `/asedit give` to get the editor tool, then right-click an armor stand to open the edit menu. You can rotate individual body parts, toggle invisibility, or lock interaction.

### Invisible item frames

Invisible item frames show only the inserted item. Obtain them with:

| Version | Command |
| --- | --- |
| Regular | `/imageframe giveinvisibleframe regular` |
| Glowing | `/imageframe giveinvisibleframe glowing` |

## Spatial menus

Sneak and press Swap Item With Offhand (default `Shift + F`) to open the spatial menu, or use `/menu`. Aim at buttons and click.

- Scroll over a list or use its page buttons. Scrolling over numeric controls adjusts their value.
- Search fields capture your next chat message; pressing Enter submits it and restores normal chat.
- Adjust menu size, text size, language, and motion in personal settings.

## Music and jukeboxes

Run `/music` for the music menu; sneak-right-click a jukebox with an empty hand for its queue. Device control requires the relevant plot permission.

Regular audio requires Plasmo Voice; NBS uses vanilla note-block sounds. In the song menu, play immediately to replace the current song, or append to the queue to wait. Music continues after you close the menu.

Multiple jukeboxes can form a synchronized group with a shared queue.

<Callout kind="warning" title="Remove physical discs first">

Manually starting or changing a digital song clears any physical disc in the jukebox without returning it. Inserting a physical disc pauses digital playback.

</Callout>

## AFK and effective playtime

Use `/afk` to go AFK, or `/afk Lunch` to set a status message. Running it again only updates or clears the message; you remain AFK.

**Press a movement key again to leave AFK.** If you were holding it when AFK began, release it first.

About three minutes without valid input triggers automatic AFK. Only chatting or turning your view for an extended period can also trigger it.

AFK time does not count as effective playtime. Teleports and movement caused by water or pistons do not count as active input. Continuous movement alone cannot keep accumulating effective playtime or activity rewards indefinitely.

## Flight and view distance

`/fly` toggles personal flight. Use `/fly speed 2` to set `2x` speed, or `/fly speed reset` to restore `1x`.

<Details title="Why did my flight speed or view distance decrease?">

- When the network is busy, the server first reduces chunk sending distance for AFK players. Active players' view distance is reduced only if pressure remains high.
- High-speed exploration during sustained network or server load can temporarily reduce your chunk range and native flight speed.
- `/fly status` shows your actual speed and, while limited, your requested speed. Your setting is preserved; limits ease gradually as load falls.

</Details>

## Everyday protection and cleanup

### Clear negative effects

In Creative or Spectator mode, receiving a negative potion effect outside PVP combat produces a chat notice with its source and cleanup buttons.

<Definitions>
<Definition title="Clear negative effects">

Remove negative effects while keeping beneficial and neutral effects.

</Definition>
<Definition title="Clear all effects">

Remove every potion effect. This option appears when beneficial or neutral effects are also present.

</Definition>
</Definitions>

Buttons expire after one minute and can be used once. Enabling PVP alone does not block cleanup; actual combat does.

### Personal trash bin

Open `/trash` or `/garbage` and place unwanted items inside. The Sort button merges matching stacks and sorts items by type.

<Callout kind="warning" title="Items remain for 10 seconds after closing">

Reopen within 10 seconds to recover misplaced items; closing again restarts the timer. Items cannot be recovered after the timer expires, you click Clear, or the server restarts.

</Callout>

### Trampling protection

Players and mobs cannot trample farmland or turtle eggs. Pressure plates and big dripleaves retain their vanilla interactions.
