Minecraft and web chat share public, private, and staff channels, so you can continue conversations across devices. QQ and Matrix also connect to public chat

## Channels and Visibility

<Definitions>
<Definition title="Public">

For players in the public channel. Minecraft and web messages are shared, with QQ and Matrix connected according to the rules below

</Definition>
<Definition title="Private">

Visible only to the two participants, including conversations between Minecraft and the web. The recipient must be online in Minecraft or web chat

</Definition>
<Definition title="Staff">

Available only to moderators and custodians. Minecraft and web staff channels share messages with authorized channel members

</Definition>
</Definitions>

## Start Chatting

<Tabs defaultValue="game">
<Tab value="game" title="Minecraft">

Your default in-game channel is public. Type a message to chat with everyone in that channel

<Definitions>
<Definition title="Public Channel">

Use `/public` to return to public chat. `/public <message>` sends one public message while keeping your current channel. The alias `/global` supports the same usage

</Definition>
<Definition title="Private Channel">

Use `/msg <player>` to switch to a private channel with that player. Run it again for the same player to return to public chat. While in private chat, `/msg` on its own also returns you to public chat

</Definition>
<Definition title="One-Time Messages and Replies">

Use `/msg <player> <message>` to send one private message, or `/r <message>` to reply to the player you last messaged. Both keep your current channel

`/tell`, `/w`, and `/whisper` are aliases of `/msg`; `/reply` is an alias of `/r`

</Definition>
</Definitions>

For example, send one public or private message while keeping your current channel:

```mcfunction
/public Hello everyone
```

```mcfunction
/msg Steve Hello
```

</Tab>
<Tab value="web" title="Web">

Sign in to [web chat](/en/chat) and choose the public channel or a private conversation. Authorized staff can also choose the staff channel. You do not need to be connected to Minecraft

- Select "New message" or choose someone from the online player list
- Send text, links, or mentions, up to 256 characters per message
- Opening the chat page connects you; leaving it disconnects you

Enable private-message and mention notifications in chat settings and grant browser notification permission. The page must stay connected; closing all chat pages stops notifications

<Details title="Where Local History Is Saved">

With "Save local history" enabled, messages, drafts, and conversations stay in the current browser. History is not shared across devices. Messages are kept for up to 30 days; disable saving or clear history in chat settings

Leaving the chat page keeps local history. Signing out or switching accounts clears it. History contains messages this browser received; messages missed while offline are not replayed

</Details>

</Tab>
</Tabs>

## Mentions and Message Content

<Tabs defaultValue="mentions">
<Tab value="mentions" title="Mentions">

In Minecraft, prefer `@ player`: a space after `@` lets you use Tab to complete the player name. `@player` also works. Names are case-insensitive; a name without `@` does not trigger a mention

```text
@ Steve Come take a look
```

On the web, typing `@` offers online players in the current channel. Minecraft public chat can also mention offline players with linked platform accounts

Adjust in-game mention alerts in Main menu → Chat Settings

Only custodians can use `@all` in-game. Web chat does not send broadcast mentions

</Tab>
<Tab value="items" title="Item Sharing">

<Definitions>
<Definition title="%i" code>

Share the item in your main hand from Minecraft. `[i]` and `[item]` also work. In-game recipients can hover to view item details

</Definition>
<Definition title="%1 ~ %36" code>

Share the item in the matching inventory slot. `%1` is the first slot; empty slots display as empty

</Definition>
</Definitions>

```text
Look at this item %i
```

Send item placeholders from Minecraft. Typing `%i` or `%1` on the web does not read your game inventory. Web and social platforms show item text according to their capabilities

</Tab>
<Tab value="links" title="Links">

URLs become clickable links. Bilibili `av` / `BV` IDs and Mojira `MC-number` references are recognized too. GitHub, Minecraft Wiki, and Matrix links receive matching link styles

You can send links from both Minecraft and the web

</Tab>
<Tab value="styles" title="Text Styles">

Members and higher can use MiniMessage in-game for colors, bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, gradients, rainbow text, and resets, for example:

```text
<gold><bold>Hello everyone</bold></gold>
```

See the [official MiniMessage format reference](https://docs.papermc.io/adventure/minimessage/format/) for syntax and more examples

Web chat displays existing message colors and decorations. Entering MiniMessage tags directly on the web does not apply those styles. In-game style tags also do not support click events, selectors, or newlines

</Tab>
</Tabs>

## Delayed Sending

Sent messages cannot be recalled. Delayed sending in Minecraft gives you a few seconds before a message actually goes out. During the wait, only you see the preview; use `/cancel` or `/c` to cancel

In Main menu → Chat Settings, choose `3s`, `5s`, or `7s` for the waiting period, or `off` to send immediately. This applies to public, staff, and private messages sent in-game. Once the wait ends and the message is sent, it cannot be recalled

## Platform Bridging

<Definitions>
<Definition title="Minecraft ↔ Web">

Public, private, and staff messages follow their own channel visibility. Messages from the web show their source

</Definition>
<Definition title="Matrix">

Ordinary messages flow automatically in both directions between the room, Minecraft public chat, and web public chat. No prefix is needed

</Definition>
<Definition title="QQ">

Ordinary messages from Group 1 reach Minecraft, web public chat, and Matrix without a prefix or bot mention. Messages from those platforms need a leading `#` to reach Group 1, such as `#Hello everyone`

Group 2 (backup) is not currently bridged

<Callout title="Forwarding, Moderation, and Privacy">

Before a message is forwarded to QQ, its text, the sender's display name, mentions, and link destinations are sent to OVHcloud for moderation with Qwen3Guard

</Callout>

</Definition>
</Definitions>

<Callout title="QQ and Matrix Receive Public Channel Messages">

Staff and private messages are not forwarded to these groups or rooms. Adding `#` to a private message does not change its channel visibility

</Callout>

## Identity and Name Tags

<Definitions>
<Definition title="Minecraft Name Tags">

Members and higher can edit prefixes and suffixes through `/nametag` or Main menu → Settings → Name Tags. Select a field, enter MiniMessage in chat, then preview and confirm. Clearing also requires a preview and confirmation

Name tags appear in chat, the tab list, and linked external messages. The vanilla player name remains above your head

</Definition>
<Definition title="Platform Identity">

Messages show their source platform. Linked QQ and Matrix accounts use their Minecraft identity; unlinked accounts use their platform nickname. See [Accounts & Community](?section=commands#accounts--community) for linking commands

</Definition>
</Definitions>
